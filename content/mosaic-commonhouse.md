<p>Mosaic Common's <a href="http://photos.mosaic-commons.org/common-house">Common House</a> is the center of our neighborhood, both physically and socially.  It's the place where the community comes together -- for shared meals, for games and entertainment, for meetings, for parties, for hobbies and activities.</p>
<p>Our Common House provides<br>
</p><ul style="margin-left:50px;">
<li>A great room for (optional) shared meals and large events
</li><li>A dining room for more private meals, or expansion space on the great room
</li><li>A large commercial-quality kitchen with mixed-height work surfaces, gas ranges, two ovens and refrigerators, and commercial dishwasher
</li><li>A kid's playroom with a window view from the building's hub, and its own bathroom and exit to the outdoors
</li><li>A recreation/multi-purpose room with ping pong, puzzles, crafts tables, foosball and more
</li><li>An exercise room
</li><li>A living room for smaller gatherings and casual socializing
</li><li>A music room
</li><li>Shared laundry facilities (in addition to laundry hookups in every home)
</li><li>A small herb garden off the kitchen, in addition to the large community vegetable garden
</li><li>A large shaded front porch
</li><li>Smaller rooms for hobbies, smaller meetings, etc.
</li><li>Mail boxes and coat room
</li><li>Handicapped accessible bathroom and shower
</li><li>A large outdoor hot tub
</li><li>A paved courtyard for outdoor cooking, patio tables and children's play
</li><li>Internet service
</li><li>Dual fuel fireplace (gas/wood)
</li><li>Energy efficient heating and cooling (heat pump)
</li><li>Super insulated and low-toxicity, just like all our individual units
</li></ul>
<p>All residents have full use of the common house.  It's like having extra rooms on your house that happens to not be attached.</p>
