<p>The Common House at Camelot Cohousing is the center of our neighborhood, both physically and socially. It's the place where the community comes together -- for shared meals, for games and entertainment, for meetings, for parties, for hobbies and activities.</p>
<h3>Our Common House provides:</h3>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<ul style="margin-left:50px;">
<li><em>Spectacular Great Room</em> for optional shared meals and large events. Features soaring clerestory ceiling, two-sided tiled fireplace, bamboo floor.</li>
<li><em>Large</em> <em>Commercial-quality Kitchen</em> with gas ranges, two ovens and refrigerators, and commercial dishwasher</li>
<li><em>Children’s Playroom</em> with window into the great room, so parents can easily “check in”</li>
<li><em>Multi-use “Dojo”</em> for dance, yoga, meditation, aerobics, music, etc.; expansion space from the great room for larger events</li>
<li><em>Cozy Living / TV room</em> for shared viewing and casual socializing</li>
<li><em>Exercise Room</em> with gym quality equipment</li>
<li><em>Four additional rooms</em> for hobbies, meetings, sewing &amp; crafts, games, etc.</li>
<li><em>Herb Garden</em> off the kitchen, in addition to large shared community garden</li>
<li><em>South facing</em> <em>deck for outdoor dining and sunning</em>, with easy access to multiple rooms</li>
<li><em>Easy access from both floors to heated swimming pool,</em> including spacious screened back deck</li>
<li><em>Built for accessibility: </em>elevator and handicapped accessible bathroom with shower</li>
<li><em>Paved courtyard</em> for outdoor cooking, patio tables and children's play</li>
<li>Entryway with mail boxes and coat area</li>
<li>Internet service</li>
<li>Low-toxicity and environmentally responsible building materials</li>
<li>Well insulated 2x6 construction &amp; lots of natural light, just like all our individual homes</li>
</ul>
<p>All residents have full use of the common house. It's like having a luxury recreational facility next door to enjoy with neighbors and friends.</p>
