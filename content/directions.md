Sawyer Hill EcoVillage's two neighborhoods are located on Village Lane (Mosaic Commons) and Village Court (Camelot Cohousing), off of Sawyer Hill Rd. in Berlin, MA, close to the intersection of I-495 and I-290.

Easiest route:

- Find your way to route 495
- Take I-495S to exit 67 for Berlin
- Turn left onto Route 62 West (Central Street)
- In half a mile, turn left onto Sawyer Hill Road (across from Berlin Farms)
- Go up and over the hill, about a mile and a half
- Turn right onto Village Lane. Please drive slowly on Village Lane.
- Follow the signs from there!
